# Supplier and Sales Playbook

## 1) Supplier Pitch Positioning
- Position role as "Tech Partner" rather than pure trader.
- Offer value: market execution + digital enablement + repeat demand signal.
- Negotiation target: better bulk terms and stable supply commitment.

## 2) Dual-Track Product Strategy
- Track A (bulk): cashflow channel, practical volume movement.
- Track B (gift/premium): trust and branding channel for higher-end positioning.

## 3) HK Sales Tier Priority
1. Tier 1 recovery buyers (fastest cash decision)
2. Tier 2 online/flexible buyers (mid-volume validation)
3. Tier 3 traditional wholesale (relationship-heavy)
4. Tier 4 major branded players (low short-term hit rate)

## 4) First-Contact Goal by Tier
- Tier 1: get immediate quote band and meeting slot
- Tier 2: get small-lot test acceptance
- Tier 3: leave sample + secure follow-up owner contact
- Tier 4: only attempt after proof package is mature

## 5) Proof Package Before Scaling
- sample photo set
- core quality statement (no overclaim)
- batch evidence path (documentation-ready)
- clear settlement expectation (prefer COD-first)

## 6) Field Communication Rules
- one store, one message thread, one summary block
- no verbal-only records for final terms
- always save final quote + payment term in text
