# Birdnest Strategy Master

## 1) Strategic Thesis
- Primary objective: build repeatable cash conversion without heavy upfront burn.
- Operating principle: prove one stable channel first, then scale.
- Hard constraint: before confirmed commitment volume (`>=300g`), keep execution in controlled mode.

## 2) Market Route Priority
1. Hong Kong B2B (fastest price discovery and turnover)
2. Taiwan controlled route (trusted local operator + limited scale)
3. Large institutional channels only after documentation and settlement discipline are stable

## 3) Product and Channel Design
- Bulk track (cashflow): dry nest for B2B movement and turnover.
- Premium/brand track (trust): limited high-quality showcase path for relationship leverage.
- Early stage focus: do not over-expand SKUs; keep clear pricing ladder and repeatability checks.

## 4) Compliance and Risk Gates
- HK gate: exporter health documentation and transaction records must be stable.
- TW gate: legal importer/operator structure must be clear before scale.
- Nitrite/quality proof gate: no batch evidence path, no expansion.
- Settlement gate: prioritize COD/short-cycle terms; avoid long receivable traps.

## 5) Execution Rhythm
- Weekly:
  - update buyer pipeline
  - review quote/settlement outcomes
  - classify targets into `A_CASH_FAST`, `B_TEST`, `C_RISK`
- Monthly:
  - review margin after logistics/risk buffer
  - decide scale up / maintain / pause

## 6) Stop-Loss Logic
- If no repeatable buyer profile emerges in one full cycle, revert to maintenance mode.
- If settlement terms drift beyond tolerance, reduce exposure to cash-first channels only.
- If realized margin cannot cover risk buffer, stop expansion and reset pricing model.

## 7) Next 30-Day Plan
- Week 1: finalize proof-pack requirements and target SKU boundaries.
- Week 2: run focused HK quote cycle with structured logs.
- Week 3: close at least one repeatable buyer pattern.
- Week 4: validate cashflow cycle and decide next-month posture.
